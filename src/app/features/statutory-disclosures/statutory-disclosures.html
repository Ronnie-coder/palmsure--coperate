2. Intermediary Disclaimer & Mandate

PALMSURE INSURANCE BROKERS acts as an independent financial intermediary. We do not underwrite insurance policies directly. We hold active mandates to represent and accept business on behalf of licensed underwriting managers and product suppliers.

3. Authorized Product Suppliers

We hold intermediary agreements with the following licensed underwriters:

  • VUM (Vantage Underwriting Management)
  • Momentum
  • SA Taxi
  • Auto & General
  • 1st for Women

4. Remuneration & Commission Structure

We earn income via regulated statutory intermediary commissions paid by product suppliers (capped at 12.5% for motor policies and 20% for non-motor policies) and agreed broker fees where applicable under short-term insurance legislation.

5. Professional Indemnity & Fidelity Cover

In full compliance with FAIS requirements, PALMSURE INSURANCE BROKERS holds active and comprehensive Professional Indemnity and Fidelity Insurance.
