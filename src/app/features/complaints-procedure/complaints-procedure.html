Complaints Procedure

Our step-by-step process for resolving client grievances in accordance with FAIS statutory requirements.

1. Internal Resolution Process

If you have a complaint regarding our advice or service, please notify us in writing with your full name, policy details, and nature of the dispute.

Email Internal Complaints:

info@palmsure.co.za

2. Turnaround Timelines

01

Acknowledgment (Within 48 Hours)

We log your complaint and send written confirmation with an assigned reference number.

02

Investigation (Within 15 Days)

Our management and compliance team reviews the claim history, recording logs, and policy terms.

03

Final Response (Max 6 Weeks)

You receive a detailed written outcome. If unresolved within 6 weeks, escalation options are provided.

3. External Escalation (NFO)

If your complaint remains unresolved after 6 weeks, you have the statutory right to escalate to the National Financial Ombud Scheme within 6 months.

  • Ombud: National Financial Ombud Scheme (NFO)
  • Email: info@nfosa.co.za
  • Phone: 0860 800 900
  • Website: www.nfosa.co.za
