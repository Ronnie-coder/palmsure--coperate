Conflict of Interest Policy

Ensuring transparent, unbiased, and fair advice across all client interactions.

1. Overview & Policy Statement

PALMSURE INSURANCE BROKERS PTY LTD (FSP 47156) maintains a strict Conflict of Interest Management Policy to identify, avoid, and mitigate any scenario that could compromise objective advice to our clients.

2. Financial Interest & Gift Limitation (R1,000 Cap)

Under the FAIS General Code of Conduct, our representatives and Key Individuals are prohibited from accepting gifts or financial incentives that create biased recommendations.

  • R1,000 Immaterial Cap: No representative may accept gifts, entertainment, or hospitality exceeding R1,000 per calendar year from any single insurer or third party.
  • Conflict Register: Every financial interest offered or received is formally audited and logged in our internal register.
